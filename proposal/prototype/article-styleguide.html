Article element styles (prototype reference)

1 Jan 2024 · By CD Bella · Example category

This is the archive or fiction note box. It sits above the article body.

Heading level 2

A paragraph of body text at 17px with a 1.65 line height and a measure of about 66 characters. It includes bold text, italic text, inline code and a link to somewhere.

Heading level 3

Unordered list:

  • First item
  • Second item
  • Third item

Ordered list:

  1. First step
  2. Second step
  3. Third step

A blockquote, used for a short quotation or an aside.
