Plant a tree, one day

Design mockup. Static, not wired to the site. Interest version only: no money, no prices, no claims that trees are planted. Copy comes from TREE_SPEC; the few words of form microcopy that the spec does not cover (error messages, "or write to") are draft.

1. Icon sheet

Four species shown by their seed or fruit, plus the sapling and the button glyph. 28px base, 56px large. On paper: forest stroke with a laterite accent. On forest-deep: --on-dark stroke with an --on-dark-mark accent.

2. Hero picker, Planting scene

A tap on the stage plants a sapling and opens the radial picker (4 buttons, 44px, each labelled). Choosing one plants that species' icon in laterite and shows a compact dismissible card near the scene bar. The "Plant a tree" text button in the scene bar is the keyboard and screen-reader route on every scene.
