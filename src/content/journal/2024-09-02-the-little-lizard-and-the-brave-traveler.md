Once upon a time, in a land of tall mountains and endless forests, there was a young traveler named Finn. Finn was on a journey to reach the top of the tallest mountain, where it was said that anyone who made it would find their true strength and happiness.

One day, as Finn was walking through the forest, a small lizard appeared and climbed onto his shoulder. The lizard whispered funny things into Finn's ear, and at first, it seemed harmless. The lizard told Finn that he didn’t need to hurry to the mountain and that it was okay to take shortcuts and ignore the challenges along the way.

As time passed, Finn noticed that the lizard was growing heavier. It wasn’t so small anymore, and its voice wasn’t just funny—it was making him feel tired and unsure. The lizard, which had once seemed like a friend, now kept him from moving forward. Every time Finn thought about continuing his journey, the lizard would convince him to rest instead.

One day, Finn met a wise old traveler who had already reached the top of the mountain. The old traveler noticed the lizard on Finn's shoulder and said, "You’re strong enough to carry that lizard, but it’s slowing you down. If you want to reach the top, you’ll need to let it go."

Finn hesitated. The lizard had been with him for so long that he wasn’t sure he could continue without it. "But what if it hurts? What if I can’t move forward without it?" Finn asked.

The old traveler smiled and said, "It may be hard, but once you let go, you’ll feel lighter, and you’ll be able to travel faster and farther than ever before. And who knows? What seems like a loss might turn into something greater."

Summoning his courage, Finn gently took the lizard from his shoulder. It squirmed and protested, but he knew it was time to let go. As soon as he set the lizard down, something magical happened—the lizard transformed into a beautiful, strong horse. Finn realized that the lizard had never been meant to hold him back; it was a part of him that needed to change.

With the horse by his side, Finn rode up the mountain with ease. He reached the top and found not just strength and happiness, but also a new understanding of himself. He learned that sometimes, the things we think we need to hold onto are the very things that keep us from growing. And when we’re brave enough to let go, we discover our true potential.

### Moral

Sometimes, the things we cling to can hold us back. By letting go of what no longer serves us, we can find new strength and achieve our dreams.
