Water is the hard part here. In the dry season there is too little. In the wet season the rain often comes three or four days in a row, and most of the land is on a slope, so the water runs straight off into the creeks.

In 2020 we tried a few small, cheap things. Most of the ideas come from permaculture.

## A swale

A swale is a shallow trench dug along the contour, with the soil heaped on the downhill side. It slows the water down and lets it soak in. We dug a small one in the garden. After heavy rain the water moved more slowly, and the soil stayed damp for three or four days longer.

## A grey water filter

Kitchen water ran through a bucket of gravel and plants before going back into the ground.

![A plastic water bottle filled with leaves on top of a bucket, piped into a second bucket of gravel with a grass plant growing in it](/images/water-2020-greywater.jpg "The grey water filter.")

## A banana circle

A pit filled with organic matter, with bananas and other water-loving plants planted around the rim. Ours takes grey water from the bathroom and the outdoor shower.

![A round pit with water in the bottom, with young banana plants around the edge](/images/water-2020-banana-circle.jpg "The banana circle.")

## A drinking water filter

Blue barrels of gravel, sand and charcoal, one feeding the next. We copied the design from the Pun Pun Center in Chiang Mai, Thailand.

![Four blue plastic barrels on a wooden stand, connected by pipes](/images/water-2020-filter.jpg "The drinking water filter.")

## A raised bed

A vegetable bed under insect netting, watered by a slow drip from a bucket.

![A long raised vegetable bed under white insect netting, with a bucket on a post feeding a drip line](/images/garden-2020-raised-bed.jpg "A covered raised bed with bucket drip, November 2020.")

## Water from the air

In July 2020 we put a small temperature and humidity sensor out at night and logged the dew point, to see how much water the air might give. It was an idea, not a working device.

![A small sensor wired to a power bank on a rock at night](/images/dew-2020-sensor.jpg "The dew point sensor, July 2020.")
