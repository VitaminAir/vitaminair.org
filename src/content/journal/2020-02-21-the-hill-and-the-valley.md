The first thing you see on the way in is the hill: a long ridge with a flat top, above dry grassland.

The road in was dirt, with a strip of fresh gravel along one side.

![A dirt road with a gravel strip running through dry grass and low trees](/images/road-2020.jpg "The road in, 11 February 2020.")

From the spot we picked for a first building, the hill shows through the trees.

![The flat-topped hill seen between tall thin trees](/images/hill-2020-from-camp.jpg "View from the building spot.")

In the mornings we looked southwest across the valley: open fields, a few huts, and mountains in the haze.

![Dry fields with a few huts and trees, and mountains in the haze behind](/images/valley-2020-southwest.jpg "Morning, looking southwest, 21 February 2020.")

The stream and the valley floor are far below.

![A steep slope with thin trees, and the valley floor far below](/images/valley-2020-below.jpg "The valley below.")

It was the dry season. Most of the grass was brown, and some patches had been burned. The [Rewilding page](/rewilding/) has a photo of the land in 2020 and again in 2024.
