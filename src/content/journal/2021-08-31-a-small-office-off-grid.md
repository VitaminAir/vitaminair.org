Electricity is a problem here. So is water, and so is the internet. That is true almost everywhere outside a city. We wanted to see how far we could get with small, cheap things.

## A path lamp

In June 2021 the office was a tent on a wooden platform, with a table under a tarp.

![A tent on a raised wooden platform under a blue tarp, with a small table and chair beside it](/images/tent-office-2021.jpg "The tent office, June 2021.")

To light the path at night without running a long mains cable through the trees, we made a lamp for under five dollars: two LEDs, a battery and a small solar panel inside a plastic bottle, on a piece of PVC pipe tied to a bamboo stick.

![A plastic bottle lamp with a small solar panel on top, tied to a bamboo stick in front of a tent](/images/lamp-2021.jpg "The path lamp.")

## The hut

By the end of August there was a small hut with a thatched roof, running on its own power:

- a 50-watt solar panel
- a 36 amp-hour battery
- a 600-watt inverter, for charging a laptop, a fan and the lights

With internet, a table, a chair, a whiteboard, a laptop and a phone, it was enough to work.

![The inside of the hut at night, lit by a lamp, with a whiteboard covered in notes](/images/office-hut-2021-inside.jpg "Inside, at night.")

![The hut lit up at night, surrounded by darkness](/images/office-hut-2021-night.jpg "The hut at night.")

The next job on the list was to clean up around it and plant flowers and fruit trees.
